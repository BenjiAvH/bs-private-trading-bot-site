Personal development project

BS Private Trading Bot

A private, non-commercial software project by Benjamin, built for research and eventual automation on his own trading accounts only.

In development · Demo-first

Purpose and intended use

The project aims to connect a locally operated research system to the developer's own broker accounts through the cTrader Open API, subject to application approval and account authorisation.

Initial integration and execution testing are intended for a demo account. The project does not manage accounts for other people.

Current status

Local data-access tools, an evidence catalogue and offline validation tests have been prepared. The authenticated broker integration is not yet operational.

No live-trading service is offered. No approval, broker partnership, profitability or investment return is claimed.

Planned broker-integration functions

  • Read market prices, historical bars and instrument specifications available to the developer's account.
  • Read the developer's own account information and trading history.
  • Test deterministic order handling on a demo account after the necessary permissions are granted.
  • Implement explicit position and loss limits, a stop mechanism and an execution journal before any separately authorised live use.

These are planned functions, not a statement that they are already implemented or approved.

Not a public financial service

This is an individual project, not a company or a client-facing investment business. There is no customer onboarding, investment advice, copy trading, subscription service or third-party account management. This website cannot log in to a broker or place orders.
